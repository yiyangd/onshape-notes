Pipe Reducer异径管

Loft two circular sections along side guides, then shell both ends open.沿侧导引线放样两个圆截面，再抽壳打通两端。

Set up Part 2 Workshop in your Onshape document在自己的 Onshape 文档中添加 Part 2 Workshop
  1. Download the workshop FeatureScript下载 Workshop 的 FeatureScript 源码
  2. Open the downloaded text file and copy its entire contents. In your Onshape practice document, click the + at the bottom left, then click Create Feature Studio.打开下载的文本文件，复制全部内容。在自己的 Onshape 练习文档左下角单击 +，再单击 Create Feature Studio。
  3. Click inside the Feature Studio editor, select all the starter code, and paste the copied text. Click Commit. Open a Part Studio in the same document and click Custom features in this workspace at the right end of the toolbar. Part 2 Workshop should appear in the list.单击 Feature Studio 编辑区，全选初始代码，粘贴复制的文本，再单击 Commit。打开同一文档中的 Part Studio，单击工具栏右端的 Custom features in this workspace，列表中应出现 Part 2 Workshop。

To practice, open your practice document and click + (Insert new tab) > Create Part Studio. Work in this new blank studio. To name it, right-click its tab, choose Rename, type a name, and press Enter. The recorded studio is called P2 Pipe Reducer.练习时打开自己的练习文档，单击 +（Insert new tab）> Create Part Studio，在新建的空白工作室中操作。若要命名，右键单击标签，选择 Rename，输入名称后按 Enter。录像中的工作室名为 P2 Pipe Reducer。

For the recorded guided Loft, start a blank Part Studio, open Part 2 Workshop, choose Pipe Reducer and 1 - Layout, then click its green check. Expand REDUCER and the Curves list. The recording in P2 Reducer - Guided Loft shows both standard Composite curve features and Loft 1.若要复现录像中的导引放样，在空白 Part Studio 中打开 Part 2 Workshop，选 Pipe Reducer 与 1 - Layout，单击绿色勾。展开 REDUCER 和 Curves 列表。工作室 P2 Reducer - Guided Loft 的录像展示两个标准 Composite curve 特征和 Loft 1。

The six Shell clips were recorded in P2 Pipe Reducer after 2 - Build. Its two cap faces are named Face of REDUCER. The same steps open both ends of Loft 1 in P2 Reducer - Guided Loft, where the two caps are listed as Face of Loft 1.六段 Shell 录像摄于 P2 Pipe Reducer，起点为 2 - Build，两个端盖面名为 Face of REDUCER。在 P2 Reducer - Guided Loft 中以相同步骤也可打通 Loft 1 的两端，两个端盖会列为 Face of Loft 1。

Open the workspace feature menu打开工作区自定义特征菜单

At the right end of the Part Studio toolbar, click the cube-shaped Custom features in this workspace dropdown. If Part 2 Workshop is missing, follow the setup instructions above.

在 Part Studio 工具栏右端，单击立方体形的 Custom features in this workspace 下拉按钮。如果列表中没有 Part 2 Workshop，请先按上方说明添加此特征。

Step 02步骤 02

Start Part 2 Workshop启动 Part 2 Workshop

Click Part 2 Workshop in the open menu. Leave the dialog open while you work through its stages.

在已打开的菜单中单击 Part 2 Workshop，保持面板打开以逐阶段查看模型。

Check · The feature dialog opens with Pyramid in Model and 1 - Layout in Stage.

核对 · 特征面板的 Model 显示 Pyramid，Stage 显示 1 - Layout。

Step 03步骤 03

Inspect the pipe reducer layout查看异径管布局

Click the top Model field and click Pipe Reducer in the list. Keep Stage at 1 - Layout. If the planes obscure the model, click blank canvas and press P to hide them. Press F to fit the geometry.

单击顶部 Model 字段，在列表中单击 Pipe Reducer。Stage 保持 1 - Layout。若基准面挡住模型，单击空白画布后按 P 将其隐藏，按 F 让几何图形适应视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pipe Reducer来源 · 录制于 P2 Pipe Reducer

Check · Two coaxial profiles measure Ø2 in and Ø1 in. The suggested practice spacing is 3 in. The guides meet both rims.

核对 · 两个同轴圆的直径为 2 in 和 1 in，建议练习间距为 3 in。两侧导引线与两圆周相交。

Step 04步骤 04

Loft the outside放样生成外形

Click the second field, Stage. In its list click 2 - Build. Wait for the feature preview to regenerate.

单击第二个字段 Stage，在列表中单击 2 - Build，等待特征预览更新。

Check · The capped reducer follows the guide curves between the wide lower end and narrow upper end.

核对 · 封闭端面的管体沿导引线从下方大端过渡至上方小端。

Step 05步骤 05

Open the ends with Shell抽壳打通两端

Click Stage again and click 3 - Finish. Leave the dialog open to inspect the result.

再次单击 Stage 并选择 3 - Finish。保持面板开启，检查预览结果。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pipe Reducer来源 · 录制于 P2 Pipe Reducer

Check · The two cap faces are removed, leaving an inward 0.1 in wall and open passages at both ends.

核对 · 上下端盖均被移除，留下向内厚 0.1 in 的管壁，两端开通。

单击 Part 2 Workshop 顶部的绿色勾。单击画布空白处，按 F 调整已保存模型的视图。

Practice with standard Onshape tools使用 Onshape 标准工具练习

Follow the sketches from the course screenshots and the recorded feature operations.参照课件截图绘制草图，跟随录像完成特征操作。

Step 07步骤 07

Sketch the wide end绘制大端截面

Click Top and Sketch, then press N. Choose Center point circle. Click Origin and then a point on its right. Press D, click the circle and place the dimension. Type 2 in, press Enter and accept the sketch.

单击 Top 和 Sketch，再按 N。选 Center point circle，单击 Origin，再单击右方一点。按 D，单击圆并放置尺寸，输入 2 in，按 Enter 并确认草图。

Source · Course page 29来源 · 课件第 29 页

Check · The lower profile is a single centered disk, 2 in in diameter.

核对 · 下截面为以原点为中心的单个圆面，直径 2 in。

Step 08步骤 08

Offset the upper plane偏移上端基准面

Select Top, click Plane and choose Offset. Enter 3 in, a suggested practice value. Press Enter, make the preview sit above the first disk and click the green check. The slide omits a legible overall height.

选择 Top，单击 Plane 并选 Offset。输入建议练习值 3 in，按 Enter。使预览面位于第一个圆面上方，再点绿色勾。课件中无法读到明确的总高度。

Source · Course page 30来源 · 课件第 30 页

Check · A parallel sketch plane is 3 in above Top.

核对 · 新草图平面与 Top 平行，高出 3 in。

Step 09步骤 09

Sketch the narrow end绘制小端截面

On Plane 1 start a new Sketch. With Center point circle click the sketch origin and place a circle. Use Dimension to set its diameter to 1 in. Press Enter and confirm the sketch.

在 Plane 1 上新建 Sketch。用 Center point circle 单击草图原点后画圆。用 Dimension 将直径设为 1 in，按 Enter 并确认草图。

Source · Course page 30来源 · 课件第 30 页

Check · The upper Ø1 in disk shares a center axis with the lower Ø2 in disk.

核对 · 上方直径 1 in 的圆面与下方直径 2 in 的圆面同轴。

Step 10步骤 10

Lay out the right guide布置右侧导引线

Start a Front sketch and press N. With Line connect (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.5,2.5) and (0.5,3) in order, in inches. Snap the first and last points to the matching circle rims, or add Pierce for rim-to-guide contact. Press Esc. This layout reserves a 0.5 in straight-end allowance at each end before blending.

在 Front 建草图并按 N。用 Line 按顺序连接 (1,0)、(1,0.5)、(0.5,2.5)、(0.5,3)，单位为 in。首尾点吸附到相应圆周，或用 Pierce 建立与圆周的接触。按 Esc。圆滑连接前，两端各预留 0.5 in 的直段。

Source · Course page 31来源 · 课件第 31 页

Check · A three-segment open guide joins the right edges of the lower and upper disks.

核对 · 一条三段开放路径连接上下圆面的右侧边缘。

Step 11步骤 11

Round and mirror the guide圆滑过渡并镜像导引线

Choose Sketch fillet and select the two segments at each transition. Enter 1 in for the radius at each corner as a suggested practice value. The radius figures on page 31 are obscured. Mirror the finished guide across a vertical construction line on the center axis, then confirm the sketch.

选 Sketch fillet，依次在每个转角选择相邻两段。两处半径可均输入建议练习值 1 in。第 31 页的半径数字被遮挡。沿中心轴画一条竖直构造线，用 Mirror 将完成的导引线镜像到左侧，然后确认草图。

Source · Course page 31来源 · 课件第 31 页

Check · Two smooth, separate side guides touch both circles symmetrically.

核对 · 两条相互独立的平滑导引线对称分布，并分别接触上下两个圆。

Pyramid四棱锥

Loft a square to a single point to form four triangular faces.将正方形放样至一个顶点，生成四个三角形侧面。

Set up Part 2 Workshop in your Onshape document在自己的 Onshape 文档中添加 Part 2 Workshop
  1. Download the workshop FeatureScript下载 Workshop 的 FeatureScript 源码
  2. Open the downloaded text file and copy its entire contents. In your Onshape practice document, click the + at the bottom left, then click Create Feature Studio.打开下载的文本文件，复制全部内容。在自己的 Onshape 练习文档左下角单击 +，再单击 Create Feature Studio。
  3. Click inside the Feature Studio editor, select all the starter code, and paste the copied text. Click Commit. Open a Part Studio in the same document and click Custom features in this workspace at the right end of the toolbar. Part 2 Workshop should appear in the list.单击 Feature Studio 编辑区，全选初始代码，粘贴复制的文本，再单击 Commit。打开同一文档中的 Part Studio，单击工具栏右端的 Custom features in this workspace，列表中应出现 Part 2 Workshop。

To practice, open your practice document and click + (Insert new tab) > Create Part Studio. Work in this new blank studio. To name it, right-click its tab, choose Rename, type a name, and press Enter. The recorded studio is called P2 Pyramid.练习时打开自己的练习文档，单击 +（Insert new tab）> Create Part Studio，在新建的空白工作室中操作。若要命名，右键单击标签，选择 Rename，输入名称后按 Enter。录像中的工作室名为 P2 Pyramid。

The second recording uses Plane, Sketch and Loft in a separate Part Studio named P2 Pyramid - Native Loft. Start the standard-tools section in another blank Part Studio to reproduce its editable feature tree.第二段录制来自独立工作室 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft，使用标准 Plane、Sketch 和 Loft。请在另一个空白 Part Studio 中开始标准工具部分，以独立复现可逐项编辑的特征树。

Build with Part 2 Workshop使用 Part 2 Workshop 建模

Follow the recorded actions in the practice Part Studio.跟随练习 Part Studio 中录制的操作。

Step 01步骤 01

Open the workspace feature menu打开工作区自定义特征菜单

At the right end of the Part Studio toolbar, click the cube-shaped Custom features in this workspace dropdown. If Part 2 Workshop is missing, follow the setup instructions above.

在 Part Studio 工具栏右端，单击立方体形的 Custom features in this workspace 下拉按钮。如果列表中没有 Part 2 Workshop，请先按上方说明添加此特征。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid

Check · The dropdown contains Part 2 Workshop.

核对 · 下拉菜单中出现 Part 2 Workshop。

Step 02步骤 02

Start Part 2 Workshop启动 Part 2 Workshop

Click Part 2 Workshop in the open menu. Leave the dialog open while you work through its stages.

在已打开的菜单中单击 Part 2 Workshop，保持面板打开以逐阶段查看模型。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid

Check · The feature dialog opens with Pyramid in Model and 1 - Layout in Stage.

核对 · 特征面板的 Model 显示 Pyramid，Stage 显示 1 - Layout。

Step 03步骤 03

Inspect the pyramid layout查看四棱锥布局

Click the top Model field and click Pyramid in the list. Keep Stage at 1 - Layout. If the planes obscure the model, click blank canvas and press P to hide them. Press F to fit the geometry.

单击顶部 Model 字段，在列表中单击 Pyramid。Stage 保持 1 - Layout。若基准面挡住模型，单击空白画布后按 P 将其隐藏，按 F 让几何图形适应视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid

Check · The base is a 5 × 5 in square centered 5 in below the Origin.

核对 · 底面是 5 × 5 in 的正方形，其中心位于 Origin 正下方 5 in。

Step 04步骤 04

Place the apex确定锥顶

Click the second field, Stage. In its list click 2 - Build. Wait for the feature preview to regenerate.

单击第二个字段 Stage，在列表中单击 2 - Build，等待特征预览更新。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid

Check · A separate point lies at the Origin above the center of the base. Look edge-on if the point overlaps the square in Top view.

核对 · 一个独立点位于底面中心正上方的 Origin 处。若顶视图中点与底面重合，可从侧面观察。

Step 05步骤 05

Loft the pyramid放样生成四棱锥

Click Stage again and click 3 - Finish. Leave the dialog open to inspect the result.

再次单击 Stage 并选择 3 - Finish。保持面板开启，检查预览结果。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid

Check · Four triangular faces meet at the tip above the square base.

核对 · 四个三角形侧面在方形底面上方汇成一个锥顶。

Step 06步骤 06

Save the pyramid保存四棱锥

Click the green check at the top of Part 2 Workshop. Click a blank part of the canvas and press F to fit the saved result.

单击 Part 2 Workshop 顶部的绿色勾。单击画布空白处，按 F 调整已保存模型的视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid

Check · The feature dialog closes and Parts (1) appears in the studio.

核对 · 面板关闭，工作室中显示 Parts (1)。

Practice with standard Onshape tools使用 Onshape 标准工具练习

Follow the recorded steps in the standard Onshape feature dialogs.跟随录像，在 Onshape 标准特征对话框中逐步操作。

Step 07步骤 07

Select Top for an offset plane选择 Top 作为偏移参考

In a blank Part Studio click Top under Default geometry in the left feature tree, then click Plane on the toolbar. Leave the plane type set to Offset.

在空白 Part Studio 的左侧特征树展开 Default geometry，单击 Top，再单击工具栏的 Plane。平面类型保持 Offset。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The Plane dialog lists Top plane in its reference field and shows Offset distance.

核对 · Plane 面板的参考栏显示 Top plane，并出现 Offset distance。

Step 08步骤 08

Enter the five-inch offset输入五英寸偏移距离

Click the numeric Offset distance box. Press Ctrl+A to select its contents, type 5 in, then press Tab to apply the value and keep the dialog open.

单击 Offset distance 数值框，按 Ctrl+A 全选，输入 5 in，再按 Tab 应用数值并保持面板打开。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · Offset distance reads 5 in and the plane preview moves.

核对 · Offset distance 显示 5 in，预览平面随之移动。

Step 09步骤 09

Place Plane 1 below Top将 Plane 1 放在 Top 下方

Click the Opposite direction arrow beside Offset distance so the preview moves below Top. Click the green check in the Plane dialog.

单击 Offset distance 旁的 Opposite direction 箭头，让预览移到 Top 下方。单击 Plane 面板的绿色勾。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · Plane 1 appears in the tree, 5 in below Top and the Origin.

核对 · 特征树出现 Plane 1，它位于 Top 和 Origin 下方 5 in。

Step 10步骤 10

Start the base sketch开始绘制底面草图

Click Plane 1 in the feature tree. Click Create new sketch on the toolbar. Leave the sketch open.

在特征树中单击 Plane 1，再单击工具栏上的 Create new sketch。保持草图打开。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · Sketch 1 opens with Plane 1 as its sketch plane.

核对 · Sketch 1 打开，其草图平面为 Plane 1。

Step 11步骤 11

Choose a centered rectangle选择中心矩形

Move the pointer over the canvas and press N to look normal to Plane 1. Click the down arrow in the Line tool group, then click Center point rectangle.

把指针移到画布上，按 N 正对 Plane 1。单击 Line 工具组的下拉箭头，再单击 Center point rectangle。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The sketch faces you squarely and the rectangle tool is active.

核对 · 草图平面正对屏幕，中心矩形工具已激活。

Step 12步骤 12

Place the center and a corner放置中心和第一个角

Click once on the sketch origin bullseye at the axes’ intersection to place the center. Release the mouse, move up and right, then click once to place a corner. Keep the live dimension box active.

单击两条草图轴交点处的原点靶心，放置矩形中心。松开鼠标，向右上方移动，再单击一次放置角点。保持动态尺寸框激活。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The rectangle straddles both axes and its horizontal dimension box is highlighted.

核对 · 矩形以两轴交点为中心，水平尺寸输入框处于高亮状态。

Step 13步骤 13

Set the width to five inches将宽度设为五英寸

With the horizontal dimension box already highlighted, type 5 and press Enter. This studio uses inches.

在水平尺寸框高亮时，直接键入 5，按 Enter。此工作室的长度单位是英寸。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The horizontal dimension reads 5 in and focus advances to the other size.

核对 · 水平尺寸为 5 in，输入焦点移至另一尺寸。

Step 14步骤 14

Set the height to five inches将高度设为五英寸

With the vertical dimension box highlighted, type 5 and press Enter. Press Esc to exit Center point rectangle.

在竖直尺寸框高亮时键入 5，按 Enter。按 Esc 退出 Center point rectangle。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The shaded base is a centered 5 × 5 in square.

核对 · 带填色的底面成为居中的 5 × 5 in 正方形。

Step 15步骤 15

Finish Sketch 1完成 Sketch 1

Click the green check in the sketch dialog. With the pointer over the canvas press Shift+1 for Front view.

单击草图面板的绿色勾。将指针移到画布，按 Shift+1 切换到 Front 视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · Sketch 1 appears below Plane 1 in the tree. In Front view the horizontal base is seen edge-on.

核对 · 特征树中的 Plane 1 下方出现 Sketch 1。Front 视图中，水平底面呈侧边形态。

Step 16步骤 16

Frame the base and apex调整视图以看清底面和锥顶

Press Shift+7 for an isometric view. If the default planes are visible, press P to hide them. Press F to fit the geometry in the canvas.

按 Shift+7 切至等轴测视图。若默认基准面仍显示，按 P 隐藏它们。按 F 使几何图形适应画布。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The square is visible at an angle with room above it for the apex.

核对 · 可以斜视完整正方形，其上方留有可见锥顶的空间。

Step 17步骤 17

Open Loft with Solid and New打开实体新建 Loft

Click the down arrow beside Extrude in the top toolbar, then click Loft. Check the blue Solid and New selections in the dialog.

单击顶部工具栏 Extrude 旁的下拉箭头，再单击 Loft。在面板中检查蓝色高亮的 Solid 和 New。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The empty Profiles field is ready to accept the square.

核对 · 空白 Profiles 字段已可选择方形截面。

Step 18步骤 18

Select the square face选择方形面

With Profiles active, move the pointer well inside the shaded square, away from its edges and corners. Click once. If an Edge entry appears, click its × and click the square interior again.

保持 Profiles 激活，将指针移入填色方形内部，避开边与角，单击一次。若出现 Edge 条目，单击该行 ×，再选方形内部。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The first profile row reads Face of Sketch 1.

核对 · 第一个截面条目显示 Face of Sketch 1。

Step 19步骤 19

Select the Origin as the apex选择 Origin 作为锥顶

Keep Profiles active and move above the square to the separate black bullseye on the canvas. Zoom toward it if needed. Click its exact center once. The dot inside the square belongs to the base sketch.

保持 Profiles 激活，向方形上方移动，在画布上找到独立的黑色原点靶心。必要时朝靶心放大，准确单击其中心一次。方形内部的圆点属于底面草图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · Vertex of Origin appears beneath Face of Sketch 1. The preview shows four triangular sides meeting at the new tip and Part 1 in the parts list.

核对 · Face of Sketch 1 下方出现 Vertex of Origin。预览的四个三角形侧面汇于新锥顶，零件列表中出现 Part 1。

Step 20步骤 20

Accept the native Loft确认标准 Loft 特征

Check the order in Profiles, with the square first and the Origin vertex second. Click the green check at the top of Loft.

确认 Profiles 中方形面在前、Origin 顶点在后，再单击 Loft 顶部的绿色勾。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pyramid - Native Loft来源 · 录制于 P2 Pyramid - Native Loft

Check · The feature tree lists Plane 1, Sketch 1 and Loft 1, and Parts (1) contains the finished pyramid.

核对 · 特征树列出 Plane 1、Sketch 1 和 Loft 1，Parts (1) 中包含完成的四棱锥。

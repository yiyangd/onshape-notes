Pawn国际象棋兵

Revolve a traced half-profile to form the pawn’s head, stem and foot.将描出的半剖面旋转，形成兵棋的头部、细颈和底座。

Set up Part 2 Workshop in your Onshape document在自己的 Onshape 文档中添加 Part 2 Workshop
  1. Download the workshop FeatureScript下载 Workshop 的 FeatureScript 源码
  2. Open the downloaded text file and copy its entire contents. In your Onshape practice document, click the + at the bottom left, then click Create Feature Studio.打开下载的文本文件，复制全部内容。在自己的 Onshape 练习文档左下角单击 +，再单击 Create Feature Studio。
  3. Click inside the Feature Studio editor, select all the starter code, and paste the copied text. Click Commit. Open a Part Studio in the same document and click Custom features in this workspace at the right end of the toolbar. Part 2 Workshop should appear in the list.单击 Feature Studio 编辑区，全选初始代码，粘贴复制的文本，再单击 Commit。打开同一文档中的 Part Studio，单击工具栏右端的 Custom features in this workspace，列表中应出现 Part 2 Workshop。

To practice, open your practice document and click + (Insert new tab) > Create Part Studio. Work in this new blank studio. To name it, right-click its tab, choose Rename, type a name, and press Enter. The recorded studio is called P2 Pawn.练习时打开自己的练习文档，单击 +（Insert new tab）> Create Part Studio，在新建的空白工作室中操作。若要命名，右键单击标签，选择 Rename，输入名称后按 Enter。录像中的工作室名为 P2 Pawn。

The saved P2 Pawn uses Part 2 Workshop at 2 - Build followed by a standard Revolve. Expand PAWN in the feature tree to see its starter sketch. The sketch instructions below also work from a blank Part Studio.已保存的 P2 Pawn 先将 Part 2 Workshop 设为 2 - Build，再接标准 Revolve。在特征树展开 PAWN 可查看起始草图。从空白 Part Studio 开始也可按下方步骤手绘剖面。

Build with Part 2 Workshop使用 Part 2 Workshop 建模

Follow the recorded actions in the practice Part Studio.跟随练习 Part Studio 中录制的操作。

Step 01步骤 01

Open the workspace feature menu打开工作区自定义特征菜单

At the right end of the Part Studio toolbar, click the cube-shaped Custom features in this workspace dropdown. If Part 2 Workshop is missing, follow the setup instructions above.

在 Part Studio 工具栏右端，单击立方体形的 Custom features in this workspace 下拉按钮。如果列表中没有 Part 2 Workshop，请先按上方说明添加此特征。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · The dropdown contains Part 2 Workshop.

核对 · 下拉菜单中出现 Part 2 Workshop。

Step 02步骤 02

Start Part 2 Workshop启动 Part 2 Workshop

Click Part 2 Workshop in the open menu. Leave the dialog open while you work through its stages.

在已打开的菜单中单击 Part 2 Workshop，保持面板打开以逐阶段查看模型。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · The feature dialog opens with Pyramid in Model and 1 - Layout in Stage.

核对 · 特征面板的 Model 显示 Pyramid，Stage 显示 1 - Layout。

Step 03步骤 03

Inspect the pawn layout查看国际象棋兵布局

Click the top Model field and click Pawn in the list. Keep Stage at 1 - Layout. If the planes obscure the model, click blank canvas and press P to hide them. Press F to fit the geometry.

单击顶部 Model 字段，在列表中单击 Pawn。Stage 保持 1 - Layout。若基准面挡住模型，单击空白画布后按 P 将其隐藏，按 F 让几何图形适应视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · The open outline shows the right side of the foot, collar and head. The suggested practice height is 2.05 in.

核对 · 开放轮廓描出底座、凸缘与头部的右侧。建议练习高度为 2.05 in。

Step 04步骤 04

Close the rotation profile闭合旋转剖面

Click the second field, Stage. In its list click 2 - Build. Wait for the feature preview to regenerate.

单击第二个字段 Stage，在列表中单击 2 - Build，等待特征预览更新。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · A vertical edge joins the crown to the bottom center, enclosing the entire right-hand profile.

核对 · 一条竖边从头顶连到底部中心，封闭整个右半剖面。

Step 05步骤 05

Revolve the pawn旋转生成棋子

Click Stage again and click 3 - Finish. Leave the dialog open to inspect the result.

再次单击 Stage 并选择 3 - Finish。保持面板开启，检查预览结果。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · A round head sits above the collar, narrow stem and flared base. The head radius is a suggested practice value of 0.29 in.

核对 · 球头位于凸缘、细颈和外展底座之上。头部半径建议练习值为 0.29 in。

Step 06步骤 06

Save the pawn保存国际象棋兵

Click the green check at the top of Part 2 Workshop. Click a blank part of the canvas and press F to fit the saved result.

单击 Part 2 Workshop 顶部的绿色勾。单击画布空白处，按 F 调整已保存模型的视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · The feature dialog closes and Parts (1) appears in the studio.

核对 · 面板关闭，工作室中显示 Parts (1)。

Practice with standard Onshape tools使用 Onshape 标准工具练习

Follow the sketches from the course screenshots and the recorded feature operations.参照课件截图绘制草图，跟随录像完成特征操作。

Step 07步骤 07

Import the pawn reference导入兵棋参考图

Open the full-size reference image below and save it as pawn-reference.png. In a fresh Part Studio, click Front and Sketch, then press N. Click Insert image. In its picker click Import and choose the saved image. Select the imported image and click Done.

打开下方的原图，将它保存为 pawn-reference.png。在新的 Part Studio 中单击 Front 和 Sketch，再按 N。单击 Insert image，在选择框中单击 Import，选择刚才保存的图片。选中导入的图片，单击 Done。

Source · Course page 9来源 · 课件第 9 页

Check · The pawn image follows the pointer, ready to place.

核对 · 兵棋参考图随指针移动，可以开始放置。

Step 08步骤 08

Place the photo for tracing放置描图参考

Drag in the sketch from the intended lower-left of the image box to its upper-right. Align the pawn’s vertical center with the sketch vertical axis and its bottom with the horizontal axis. The reference image may include a dark margin. Align the piece itself.

在画布上从图框的预定左下角拖到右上角。让兵棋自身的中轴与草图竖轴重合，棋底落在水平轴上。参考图带有深色边缘，应以棋子轮廓定位。

Source · Course page 11来源 · 课件第 11 页

Check · You can see both the right-hand silhouette and the centerline from base to crown.

核对 · 可以同时看清右侧轮廓以及从底部到顶部的中轴。

Step 09步骤 09

Draw the axis and bottom radius绘制轴线和底部半径

Choose Line. Click the center of the crown, then the center of the bottom and then the outer right corner of the base. Press Esc. Leave the vertical segment as ordinary sketch geometry so it will also close the profile. Set its length with Dimension to 2.05 in, a suggested practice value.

选 Line。依次单击头顶中心、底部中心和底座最右下角，按 Esc。竖线保持为普通草图线，用于封闭轮廓。用 Dimension 将其长度设为 2.05 in，这是建议练习值。

Source · Course page 12来源 · 课件第 12 页

Check · An L-shaped pair of connected edges establishes the rotation axis and bottom of the profile.

核对 · 相连的两条线构成 L 形，分别确定旋转轴和剖面底边。

Step 10步骤 10

Trace the pedestal描出分层底座

Choose 3 point arc. Click the right end of the base, click the upper end of the rounded foot, then click its outer bulge to set curvature. Repeat from that endpoint for each rounded step up to the stem. Snap each start to the previous endpoint.

选 3 point arc。单击底边右端、圆脚上端，再单击外凸点设定弧度。从已有端点继续逐段描出到细颈下方的底座圆弧，每段起点吸附到上一段终点。

Source · Course pages 11–12来源 · 课件第 11–12 页

Check · The traced edges follow the right side of the base without gaps.

核对 · 连续边沿着底座右侧轮廓延伸，接合处没有间隙。

Step 11步骤 11

Trace the stem and collar描出细颈和凸缘

Continue with 3 point arc. For the stem, click its bottom and top endpoints and a point along its inward curve. Repeat on the collar, adding one arc for the lower flare and one for its upper return.

继续用 3 point arc。先单击细颈的下、上端点，再单击内收弧上的一点。沿凸缘重复此操作，分别绘制下方外展与上方回收的弧。

Source · Course page 12来源 · 课件第 12 页

Check · The outline narrows into the stem and widens again beneath the head.

核对 · 轮廓先收窄成颈，再在球头下方伸出凸缘。

Step 12步骤 12

Close the ball head闭合球头轮廓

Choose 3 point arc. Click the collar’s top-right endpoint, the crown point at the top of the vertical axis, then the far-right side of the round head. Press Esc. Use a head radius of about 0.29 in as a suggested practice value.

选 3 point arc。单击凸缘右上端、竖直轴顶端的头顶点，再单击球头最右侧设弧。按 Esc。头部半径可用约 0.29 in，作为建议练习值。

Source · Course page 12来源 · 课件第 12 页

Check · A single enclosed region fills the right half of the pawn. Zoom to any unfilled joint and join its endpoints with Coincident.

核对 · 棋子的右半边形成一个有填色的闭合区域。若未填色，放大断点并用 Coincident 重合两端点。

Step 13步骤 13

Open the native Revolve打开标准 Revolve

From the hand-drawn route, finish the sketch with its green check. With the closed half-profile visible, click the dropdown beside the first solid feature in the toolbar, usually Extrude. Click Revolve. Keep Solid, New and Full selected.

若从手绘路线开始，先单击绿色勾完成草图。闭合半剖面可见时，单击工具栏第一个实体特征（通常为 Extrude）旁的下拉箭头，再单击 Revolve。保持选中 Solid、New 和 Full。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · The Revolve dialog is open and its profile selection is ready.

核对 · Revolve 面板打开，剖面选择栏可用。

Step 14步骤 14

Select the half-profile face选择半剖面区域

With Faces and sketch regions to revolve active, click inside the shaded lower portion of the right-hand outline. Leave a little space between the pointer and the curved pedestal edge.

保持 Faces and sketch regions to revolve 激活，单击右半轮廓下部的填色区。让指针与弯曲的底座边缘保持一点距离。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · The selected profile reads Face of Sketch 1.

核对 · 已选剖面显示 Face of Sketch 1。

Step 15步骤 15

Select the straight rotation axis选择笔直的旋转轴

Click the Revolve axis field. Click midway along the straight vertical left boundary of the half-profile, away from either endpoint. If the selected row references a curved edge, clear its × and select the straight edge again.

单击 Revolve axis 输入栏。沿半剖面左侧笔直的竖边单击中部，避开两端点。若误选弧边，单击该行 × 后重新选择直边。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · Revolve axis reads Edge of Sketch 1 and a full pawn appears in the preview.

核对 · Revolve axis 中显示 Edge of Sketch 1，预览呈现完整棋子。

Step 16步骤 16

Accept the revolved pawn保存旋转生成的棋子

Click the green check at the top of Revolve. With the pointer over the canvas press Shift+7 for isometric view, then F to fit the model.

单击 Revolve 顶部的绿色勾。指针在画布上时按 Shift+7 切换至等轴测视图，再按 F 让模型适应画布。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pawn来源 · 录制于 P2 Pawn

Check · Revolve 1 appears in the feature tree and Parts (1) contains the completed pawn.

核对 · 特征树中出现 Revolve 1，Parts (1) 中是完成的兵棋实体。

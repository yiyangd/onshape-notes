Coffee Cup咖啡杯

Revolve the hollow bowl and sweep an elliptical section around the handle.旋转生成中空杯身，再沿杯柄路径扫掠椭圆截面。

Set up Part 2 Workshop in your Onshape document在自己的 Onshape 文档中添加 Part 2 Workshop
  1. Download the workshop FeatureScript下载 Workshop 的 FeatureScript 源码
  2. Open the downloaded text file and copy its entire contents. In your Onshape practice document, click the + at the bottom left, then click Create Feature Studio.打开下载的文本文件，复制全部内容。在自己的 Onshape 练习文档左下角单击 +，再单击 Create Feature Studio。
  3. Click inside the Feature Studio editor, select all the starter code, and paste the copied text. Click Commit. Open a Part Studio in the same document and click Custom features in this workspace at the right end of the toolbar. Part 2 Workshop should appear in the list.单击 Feature Studio 编辑区，全选初始代码，粘贴复制的文本，再单击 Commit。打开同一文档中的 Part Studio，单击工具栏右端的 Custom features in this workspace，列表中应出现 Part 2 Workshop。

To practice, open your practice document and click + (Insert new tab) > Create Part Studio. Work in this new blank studio. To name it, right-click its tab, choose Rename, type a name, and press Enter. The recorded studio is called P2 Coffee Cup.练习时打开自己的练习文档，单击 +（Insert new tab）> Create Part Studio，在新建的空白工作室中操作。若要命名，右键单击标签，选择 Rename，输入名称后按 Enter。录像中的工作室名为 P2 Coffee Cup。

The saved P2 Coffee Cup uses Part 2 Workshop at 2 - Build followed by a standard Sweep. In the starter feature, Sketch 2 contains the ellipse and Sketch 1 contains the handle path. In a blank Part Studio, identify each sketch by its geometry.已保存的 P2 Coffee Cup 先将 Part 2 Workshop 设为 2 - Build，再使用标准 Sweep。起始特征中的 Sketch 2 是椭圆截面，Sketch 1 是杯柄路径。从空白 Part Studio 手绘时，应按几何内容识别所需草图。

Build with Part 2 Workshop使用 Part 2 Workshop 建模

Follow the recorded actions in the practice Part Studio.跟随练习 Part Studio 中录制的操作。

Step 01步骤 01

Open the workspace feature menu打开工作区自定义特征菜单

At the right end of the Part Studio toolbar, click the cube-shaped Custom features in this workspace dropdown. If Part 2 Workshop is missing, follow the setup instructions above.

在 Part Studio 工具栏右端，单击立方体形的 Custom features in this workspace 下拉按钮。如果列表中没有 Part 2 Workshop，请先按上方说明添加此特征。

Step 02步骤 02

Start Part 2 Workshop启动 Part 2 Workshop

Click Part 2 Workshop in the open menu. Leave the dialog open while you work through its stages.

在已打开的菜单中单击 Part 2 Workshop，保持面板打开以逐阶段查看模型。

核对 · 特征面板的 Model 显示 Pyramid，Stage 显示 1 - Layout。

Step 03步骤 03

Inspect the coffee cup layout查看咖啡杯布局

Click the top Model field and click Coffee Cup in the list. Keep Stage at 1 - Layout. If the planes obscure the model, click blank canvas and press P to hide them. Press F to fit the geometry.

单击顶部 Model 字段，在列表中单击 Coffee Cup。Stage 保持 1 - Layout。若基准面挡住模型，单击空白画布后按 P 将其隐藏，按 F 让几何图形适应视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Coffee Cup来源 · 录制于 P2 Coffee Cup

Check · The closed wall section is 1.25 in high. It includes the 0.1 in floor and the rim.

核对 · 闭合杯壁截面高 1.25 in，包括 0.1 in 厚的底部和杯口。

Step 04步骤 04

Form the bowl and handle path生成杯身和杯柄路径

Click the second field, Stage. In its list click 2 - Build. Wait for the feature preview to regenerate.

单击第二个字段 Stage，在列表中单击 2 - Build，等待特征预览更新。

Source · Recorded in P2 Coffee Cup来源 · 录制于 P2 Coffee Cup

Check · The open bowl appears beside the curved handle path. The handle profile has 0.13 × 0.10 in full axes on a plane offset 0.62 in from Right.

核对 · 敞口杯身旁出现弯曲杯柄路径。杯柄截面的整轴尺寸为 0.13 × 0.10 in，位于距 Right 平面 0.62 in 的偏移平面上。

Step 05步骤 05

Join the swept handle连接扫掠生成的杯柄

Click Stage again and click 3 - Finish. Leave the dialog open to inspect the result.

再次单击 Stage 并选择 3 - Finish。保持面板开启，检查预览结果。

核对 · 杯柄围成指孔，两端均接入杯身。

单击 Part 2 Workshop 顶部的绿色勾。单击画布空白处，按 F 调整已保存模型的视图。

Practice with standard Onshape tools使用 Onshape 标准工具练习

Follow the sketches from the course screenshots and the recorded feature operations.参照课件截图绘制草图，跟随录像完成特征操作。

Step 07步骤 07

Draw the floor of the cup绘制杯底剖面

Click Front, Sketch and press N. Choose Line. Click Origin and draw a 0.5 in horizontal line to the right for the outside floor. From the vertical axis 0.1 in above Origin draw another 0.5 in line to the right. Use Dimension to set each length and elevation. Connect their left ends with Line.

单击 Front、Sketch，按 N。选 Line，从 Origin 向右画 0.5 in 水平线作为杯底外边。在竖轴上方 0.1 in 处再向右画 0.5 in 长的线。用 Dimension 设置线长与高度，再用 Line 连接两个左端点。

Source · Course page 21来源 · 课件第 21 页

Check · The floor section is 0.1 in thick and meets the vertical rotation axis.

核对 · 杯底截面厚 0.1 in，左侧紧贴旋转轴。

Step 08步骤 08

Place the open rim定位杯口

Add a short horizontal line at z = 1.25 in from x = 0.5 in to x = 0.6 in, with the origin at the center of the cup’s underside. Press D to dimension the line length to 0.1 in, then dimension its height from Origin. Press Enter after each value.

以杯底中心为原点，在 z = 1.25 in 处，从 x = 0.5 in 到 x = 0.6 in 画一小段水平线。按 D 将线长设为 0.1 in，并标注其距 Origin 的高度。每次输入后按 Enter。

Source · Course page 21来源 · 课件第 21 页

Check · The short rim segment lies above the two floor lines, entirely on the right of the axis.

核对 · 短杯口边位于杯底两线之上，完全处在中轴右侧。

Step 09步骤 09

Close the curved wall闭合弧形杯壁

Choose 3 point arc. Click the outside floor’s right endpoint, the rim’s right endpoint, then a point near (0.74, 0.68) in for the outward bow. Repeat from the inside floor’s right endpoint to the rim’s left endpoint through (0.634, 0.68) in. The two middle points are suggested practice values. Confirm the sketch.

选 3 point arc，依次单击杯底外边右端、杯口右端，再在约 (0.74, 0.68) in 处单击，使杯壁向外鼓起。从杯底内边右端连到杯口左端，让弧通过 (0.634, 0.68) in。两个中间点均为建议练习值。确认草图。

Source · Course page 21来源 · 课件第 21 页

Check · Only the material of the floor and wall forms a closed sketch region.

核对 · 杯底和杯壁材料形成一个闭合的填色区域。

Step 10步骤 10

Revolve the hollow cup旋转生成中空杯身

Click Revolve, Solid and New. Click the shaded wall region. Activate Revolve axis, then click the 0.1 in vertical segment connecting the floor lines at the Origin. Choose Full and click the green check.

单击 Revolve、Solid、New，单击杯壁填色区。在 Revolve axis 中选择 Origin 处连接内外底边的 0.1 in 竖线。选择 Full，单击绿色勾。

Source · Course page 22来源 · 课件第 22 页

Check · The open mouth reveals a cavity with a closed floor.

核对 · 从敞开的杯口可看到内腔和闭合的杯底。

Step 11步骤 11

Sketch the handle path绘制杯柄路径

Start another Front sketch and press N. The slide shows a freeform spline. To reproduce the round practice handle, use 3 point arc. Click (0.62, 0.26) in at the lower attachment, (0.62, 1.08) in at the upper attachment, then (1.03, 0.67) in to bow it to the right. Upper height and outer bow are suggested practice values. Confirm the sketch.

在 Front 新建草图并按 N。课件中的杯柄路径是样条曲线。若要复现练习版圆弧杯柄，请选 3 point arc，先点下端 (0.62, 0.26) in、上端 (0.62, 1.08) in，再点右侧 (1.03, 0.67) in 形成外弧。上端高度和外凸位置为建议练习值。确认草图。

Source · Course page 23来源 · 课件第 23 页

Check · The path begins inside the lower wall and returns into the upper wall, leaving an opening for a finger.

核对 · 路径从下段杯壁内部出发，再回到上段杯壁内部，中间留有指孔。

Step 12步骤 12

Create the section plane建立杯柄截面平面

Select Right, click Plane and choose Offset. Enter 0.62 in and press Enter. Flip the arrow if needed until the plane passes through the lower path endpoint on the handle side. Click the green check.

选择 Right，单击 Plane 并选 Offset。输入 0.62 in，按 Enter。按需翻转箭头，使平面经过杯柄侧的路径下端点，单击绿色勾。

Source · Course page 24来源 · 课件第 24 页

Check · Plane 1 intersects the start of the handle and is parallel to Right.

核对 · Plane 1 经过杯柄起点，并与 Right 平行。

Step 13步骤 13

Size the handle’s elliptical section确定杯柄椭圆截面

Click Plane 1 and Sketch. With Ellipse, click the lower endpoint as the center, click straight above it for the vertical major axis, then click to the side for the minor axis. Dimension the full axes to 0.13 in vertically and 0.10 in horizontally. If an input shows a radius, use half the corresponding full axis. Confirm the sketch.

单击 Plane 1 和 Sketch。选 Ellipse，在路径下端点单击放置中心，再点正上方确定竖直长轴，最后点侧方确定短轴。标注整轴长度，竖向 0.13 in、横向 0.10 in。若输入框显示半径，则填对应整轴的一半。确认草图。

Source · Course page 25来源 · 课件第 25 页

Check · The elliptical profile is centered 0.26 in above the base at the starting end of the path.

核对 · 椭圆截面中心位于路径起点，距杯底 0.26 in。

Step 14步骤 14

Open the native handle Sweep打开杯柄的标准 Sweep

With the bowl, handle path and elliptical section visible, click the dropdown beside Extrude in the toolbar and choose Sweep. Expand the starter feature in the tree if its sketches are hidden.

使杯身、杯柄路径和椭圆截面可见，单击工具栏 Extrude 旁的下拉箭头并选择 Sweep。若起始特征中的草图折叠，可在特征树中展开。

Source · Recorded in P2 Coffee Cup来源 · 录制于 P2 Coffee Cup

Check · The Sweep dialog opens with the profile field active.

核对 · Sweep 面板打开，截面字段处于激活状态。

Step 15步骤 15

Select the elliptical cross-section选择椭圆截面

With Faces and sketch regions to sweep active, click Sketch 2 in the recorded starter’s feature tree. For a hand-drawn model, select the sketch containing the 0.13 × 0.10 in ellipse on the offset plane. Sketch numbers depend on the order in which you created them.

保持 Faces and sketch regions to sweep 激活，在录像所用起始特征的树中单击 Sketch 2。对于手绘模型，应选择偏移平面上包含 0.13 × 0.10 in 椭圆的草图；实际草图编号取决于创建顺序。

Source · Recorded in P2 Coffee Cup来源 · 录制于 P2 Coffee Cup

Check · The profile selection identifies the elliptical sketch region at the lower handle attachment.

核对 · 截面字段中已选中杯柄下方接点处的椭圆区域。

Step 16步骤 16

Sweep the ellipse into the cup wall沿路径扫掠并接入杯壁

Click Sweep path, then click Sketch 1 in the recorded starter’s tree. For your own sketch, select the open arc leading out from the lower wall and back into the upper wall. Check the automatically selected Add operation and Part 1 in Merge scope. If needed, choose Add and select the bowl as the target.

单击 Sweep path，再单击录像起始特征树中的 Sketch 1。手绘模型应选从下段杯壁出发、回到上段杯壁的开放弧线。检查自动选中的 Add 及 Merge scope 中的 Part 1。必要时手动选 Add，并选择杯身为合并目标。

Source · Recorded in P2 Coffee Cup来源 · 录制于 P2 Coffee Cup

Check · The path row reads Edges of Sketch 1 in the recorded model. A solid handle meets the bowl at both ends.

核对 · 录像中的路径行显示 Edges of Sketch 1，预览为两端均接入杯身的实体杯柄。

Save the joined coffee cup保存杯柄与杯身相连的咖啡杯

单击 Sweep 顶部绿色勾。查看杯口以及杯柄与弧形杯壁连接的两处位置。

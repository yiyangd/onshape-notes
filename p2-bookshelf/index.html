Bookshelf书架

Build nine boards as separate parts, then insert the case into a rigid Assembly.将九块板件建为独立零件，再把柜体作为刚性组件插入 Assembly。

Set up Part 2 Workshop in your Onshape document在自己的 Onshape 文档中添加 Part 2 Workshop
  1. Download the workshop FeatureScript下载 Workshop 的 FeatureScript 源码
  2. Open the downloaded text file and copy its entire contents. In your Onshape practice document, click the + at the bottom left, then click Create Feature Studio.打开下载的文本文件，复制全部内容。在自己的 Onshape 练习文档左下角单击 +，再单击 Create Feature Studio。
  3. Click inside the Feature Studio editor, select all the starter code, and paste the copied text. Click Commit. Open a Part Studio in the same document and click Custom features in this workspace at the right end of the toolbar. Part 2 Workshop should appear in the list.单击 Feature Studio 编辑区，全选初始代码，粘贴复制的文本，再单击 Commit。打开同一文档中的 Part Studio，单击工具栏右端的 Custom features in this workspace，列表中应出现 Part 2 Workshop。

To practice, open your practice document and click + (Insert new tab) > Create Part Studio. Work in this new blank studio. To name it, right-click its tab, choose Rename, type a name, and press Enter. The recorded studio is called P2 Bookshelf.练习时打开自己的练习文档，单击 +（Insert new tab）> Create Part Studio，在新建的空白工作室中操作。若要命名，右键单击标签，选择 Rename，输入名称后按 Enter。录像中的工作室名为 P2 Bookshelf。

A second recorded build in P2 Bookshelf - Board Extrudes starts from a blank Part Studio. Shelf panel outlines supplies five sketches. You make all nine boards with five standard Extrude features. Both custom tools are included in Part 2 Workshop.fs.txt.第二次录制在 P2 Bookshelf - Board Extrudes 的空白工作室开始。Shelf panel outlines 提供五幅草图，你用五项标准 Extrude 制作全部九块板。两个自定义工具均包含在 Part 2 Workshop.fs.txt 中。

The finished exterior measures 24.75 in wide, 47.5 in high and 9.5 in deep. The course supplies those dimensions. For the board layout, the 0.75 in panel thickness, 0.25 in back, 0.25 in plinth setback and shelf elevations are suggested practice values. The recorded board steps give each outline’s size and position so you can also draw the rectangles yourself.成品外宽 24.75 in、总高 47.5 in、总深 9.5 in，这三项来自课件。板件布局中的 0.75 in 板厚、0.25 in 背板厚、0.25 in 踢脚板内缩量及层板高度均为建议练习值。下方录制步骤给出每组轮廓的尺寸与位置，也可据此自行绘制矩形。

Build with Part 2 Workshop使用 Part 2 Workshop 建模

Follow the recorded actions in the practice Part Studio.跟随练习 Part Studio 中录制的操作。

Step 01步骤 01

Open the workspace feature menu打开工作区自定义特征菜单

At the right end of the Part Studio toolbar, click the cube-shaped Custom features in this workspace dropdown. If Part 2 Workshop is missing, follow the setup instructions above.

在 Part Studio 工具栏右端，单击立方体形的 Custom features in this workspace 下拉按钮。如果列表中没有 Part 2 Workshop，请先按上方说明添加此特征。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf

Check · The dropdown contains Part 2 Workshop.

核对 · 下拉菜单中出现 Part 2 Workshop。

Step 02步骤 02

Start Part 2 Workshop启动 Part 2 Workshop

Click Part 2 Workshop in the open menu. Leave the dialog open while you work through its stages.

在已打开的菜单中单击 Part 2 Workshop，保持面板打开以逐阶段查看模型。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf

Check · The feature dialog opens with Pyramid in Model and 1 - Layout in Stage.

核对 · 特征面板的 Model 显示 Pyramid，Stage 显示 1 - Layout。

Step 03步骤 03

Inspect the bookshelf layout查看书架布局

Click the top Model field and click Bookshelf in the list. Keep Stage at 1 - Layout. If the planes obscure the model, click blank canvas and press P to hide them. Press F to fit the geometry.

单击顶部 Model 字段，在列表中单击 Bookshelf。Stage 保持 1 - Layout。若基准面挡住模型，单击空白画布后按 P 将其隐藏，按 F 让几何图形适应视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf

Check · Two side panels establish the 24.75 in outside width and 47.5 in height. The suggested panel thickness is 0.75 in.

核对 · 两块侧板确定 24.75 in 的外宽与 47.5 in 的总高，建议板厚为 0.75 in。

Step 04步骤 04

Build the case生成柜体框架

Click the second field, Stage. In its list click 2 - Build. Wait for the feature preview to regenerate.

单击第二个字段 Stage，在列表中单击 2 - Build，等待特征预览更新。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf

Check · The back, top, bottom and recessed plinth join the two sides as four additional parts. Overall depth is 9.5 in.

核对 · 在两块侧板之外可见背板、顶板、底板和内缩踢脚板四个独立零件，总深为 9.5 in。

Step 05步骤 05

Add three interior shelves加入三块中间层板

Click Stage again and click 3 - Finish. Leave the dialog open to inspect the result.

再次单击 Stage 并选择 3 - Finish。保持面板开启，检查预览结果。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf

Check · Three shelves divide the case into four openings. Each clear height is 10.5 in in this practice design.

核对 · 三块层板将柜体分为四个开口，本练习方案每格净高为 10.5 in。

Step 06步骤 06

Save the bookshelf保存书架

Click the green check at the top of Part 2 Workshop. Click a blank part of the canvas and press F to fit the saved result.

单击 Part 2 Workshop 顶部的绿色勾。单击画布空白处，按 F 调整已保存模型的视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf

Check · The feature dialog closes and Parts (9) appears in the studio.

核对 · 面板关闭，工作室中显示 Parts (9)。

Step 07步骤 07

Create a bookshelf assembly新建书架装配

At the bottom left click + (Insert new tab), then click Create Assembly.

在左下方单击 +（Insert new tab），再单击 Create Assembly。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf assembly来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf 装配

Check · A new Assembly tab opens with an empty Instances list.

核对 · 新的 Assembly 标签打开，Instances 列表为空。

Step 08步骤 08

Open the Insert panel打开 Insert 面板

Click Insert in the Assembly toolbar. In the panel use the Current document view to locate the Part Studios in your practice document.

单击 Assembly 工具栏的 Insert。在面板中使用 Current document 视图，查找自己练习文档内的各个 Part Studio。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf assembly来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf 装配

Check · The panel shows Part Studios from the current document.

核对 · 面板中显示当前文档的 Part Studio。

Step 09步骤 09

Choose rigid insertion选择刚性插入

At the right above the Insert results, click Insert entire Part Studio as rigid. Hover to read the tooltip if the button shows only an icon.

在 Insert 结果列表上方的右端单击 Insert entire Part Studio as rigid。若按钮仅显示图标，可将鼠标停在上方查看提示。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf assembly来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf 装配

Check · Rigid Part Studio insertion is active before a project is selected.

核对 · 在选择模型之前，整个 Part Studio 的刚性插入模式已启用。

Step 10步骤 10

Select the complete bookshelf选择完整书架

Click the P2 Bookshelf row once in the Insert results. Leave the pointer in the dialog so the original frame and shelf positions are retained.

在 Insert 结果中单击一次 P2 Bookshelf 行。将指针留在面板内，以保留柜体和层板的原始放置位置。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf assembly来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf 装配

Check · The case and its three interior shelves appear as one pending assembly instance.

核对 · 柜体及三块层板作为一个待插入实例出现。

Step 11步骤 11

Accept the rigid assembly at the origin在原点确认刚性装配

Click the green check in Insert while the pointer is still in the panel. Do not click the canvas to place a second instance.

将指针保持在面板内，单击 Insert 的绿色勾。直接在面板中确认即可按原点放置。

Source · Recorded in P2 Bookshelf assembly来源 · 录制于 P2 Bookshelf 装配

Check · Instances (1) contains the rigid bookshelf aligned to the assembly origin.

核对 · Instances (1) 包含一个刚性书架实例，并与装配原点对齐。

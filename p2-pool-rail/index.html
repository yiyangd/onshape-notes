Pool Rail泳池扶手

Sweep a circular section along an open path with three tangent bends.沿含三处相切弯道的开放路径扫掠圆形截面。

Set up Part 2 Workshop in your Onshape document在自己的 Onshape 文档中添加 Part 2 Workshop
  1. Download the workshop FeatureScript下载 Workshop 的 FeatureScript 源码
  2. Open the downloaded text file and copy its entire contents. In your Onshape practice document, click the + at the bottom left, then click Create Feature Studio.打开下载的文本文件，复制全部内容。在自己的 Onshape 练习文档左下角单击 +，再单击 Create Feature Studio。
  3. Click inside the Feature Studio editor, select all the starter code, and paste the copied text. Click Commit. Open a Part Studio in the same document and click Custom features in this workspace at the right end of the toolbar. Part 2 Workshop should appear in the list.单击 Feature Studio 编辑区，全选初始代码，粘贴复制的文本，再单击 Commit。打开同一文档中的 Part Studio，单击工具栏右端的 Custom features in this workspace，列表中应出现 Part 2 Workshop。

To practice, open your practice document and click + (Insert new tab) > Create Part Studio. Work in this new blank studio. To name it, right-click its tab, choose Rename, type a name, and press Enter. The recorded studio is called P2 Pool Rail.练习时打开自己的练习文档，单击 +（Insert new tab）> Create Part Studio，在新建的空白工作室中操作。若要命名，右键单击标签，选择 Rename，输入名称后按 Enter。录像中的工作室名为 P2 Pool Rail。

The saved P2 Pool Rail uses Part 2 Workshop at 2 - Build followed by a standard Sweep. Expand RAIL in the feature tree to see its starter sketches. The sketch instructions below also work from a blank Part Studio.已保存的 P2 Pool Rail 先将 Part 2 Workshop 设为 2 - Build，再接标准 Sweep。在特征树展开 RAIL 可查看起始草图。从空白 Part Studio 开始也可按下方步骤手绘两幅草图。

Build with Part 2 Workshop使用 Part 2 Workshop 建模

Follow the recorded actions in the practice Part Studio.跟随练习 Part Studio 中录制的操作。

Step 01步骤 01

Open the workspace feature menu打开工作区自定义特征菜单

At the right end of the Part Studio toolbar, click the cube-shaped Custom features in this workspace dropdown. If Part 2 Workshop is missing, follow the setup instructions above.

在 Part Studio 工具栏右端，单击立方体形的 Custom features in this workspace 下拉按钮。如果列表中没有 Part 2 Workshop，请先按上方说明添加此特征。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The dropdown contains Part 2 Workshop.

核对 · 下拉菜单中出现 Part 2 Workshop。

Step 02步骤 02

Start Part 2 Workshop启动 Part 2 Workshop

Click Part 2 Workshop in the open menu. Leave the dialog open while you work through its stages.

在已打开的菜单中单击 Part 2 Workshop，保持面板打开以逐阶段查看模型。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The feature dialog opens with Pyramid in Model and 1 - Layout in Stage.

核对 · 特征面板的 Model 显示 Pyramid，Stage 显示 1 - Layout。

Step 03步骤 03

Inspect the pool rail layout查看泳池扶手布局

Click the top Model field and click Pool Rail in the list. Keep Stage at 1 - Layout. If the planes obscure the model, click blank canvas and press P to hide them. Press F to fit the geometry.

单击顶部 Model 字段，在列表中单击 Pool Rail。Stage 保持 1 - Layout。若基准面挡住模型，单击空白画布后按 P 将其隐藏，按 F 让几何图形适应视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The Front-plane path has two open feet and three bends with diameters 1, 0.6 and 0.75 in.

核对 · Front 平面上的路径有两个开放脚端。三处弯道的直径分别为 1、0.6 和 0.75 in。

Step 04步骤 04

Place the tube section放置管材截面

Click the second field, Stage. In its list click 2 - Build. Wait for the feature preview to regenerate.

单击第二个字段 Stage，在列表中单击 2 - Build，等待特征预览更新。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · A Ø0.10 in circular profile sits horizontally at the foot of the tall leg, perpendicular to the path.

核对 · 直径 0.10 in 的圆形截面水平放置在高腿脚端，与路径垂直。

Step 05步骤 05

Sweep the round rail扫掠生成圆形扶手

Click Stage again and click 3 - Finish. Leave the dialog open to inspect the result.

再次单击 Stage 并选择 3 - Finish。保持面板开启，检查预览结果。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The same round section follows the sloping top, left return and short leg without a gap.

核对 · 同一个圆截面沿斜顶、左侧折返弯和短腿延伸，全程连续。

Step 06步骤 06

Save the pool rail保存泳池扶手

Click the green check at the top of Part 2 Workshop. Click a blank part of the canvas and press F to fit the saved result.

单击 Part 2 Workshop 顶部的绿色勾。单击画布空白处，按 F 调整已保存模型的视图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The feature dialog closes and Parts (1) appears in the studio.

核对 · 面板关闭，工作室中显示 Parts (1)。

Practice with standard Onshape tools使用 Onshape 标准工具练习

Follow the sketches from the course screenshots and the recorded feature operations.参照课件截图绘制草图，跟随录像完成特征操作。

Step 07步骤 07

Lay out the bend centers定位弯管中心

Click Front, Sketch and then press N. Treat the origin as (0,0), right as +x and up as +z. Choose Center point circle and place three circles roughly at the centers in the drawing. For each circle click its center, then a point on its circumference. Press Esc.

单击 Front、Sketch，再按 N。令原点为 (0,0)，向右为 +x，向上为 +z。选 Center point circle，依图大致放置三个圆。每个圆先点圆心，再点圆周，最后按 Esc。

Source · Course page 16来源 · 课件第 16 页

Check · Three separate circles mark the bends and leave room for connecting lines.

核对 · 三个独立圆表示三处弯管，彼此之间留有连接直线的空间。

Step 08步骤 08

Dimension the three circles标注三个圆

Press D, click a circle and click outside it to place a diameter. Enter 1 in for the upper-right circle, 0.6 in for the far-left circle and 0.75 in for the lower-inner circle. Press Enter after each entry.

按 D，单击圆，再单击圆外放置直径尺寸。右上圆输入 1 in，最左圆输入 0.6 in，内部下方圆输入 0.75 in。每次输入后按 Enter。

Source · Course page 16来源 · 课件第 16 页

Check · The three diameter labels agree with the Front-view drawing.

核对 · 三个直径标注与正视图一致。

Step 09步骤 09

Locate each center from the origin从原点定位各圆心

Set the upper-right center on the vertical axis at z = 1.4 in. Locate the left center at (−2, 1.05) in and the inner center at (−1, 0.375) in. For each offset press D, click Origin and the center, move above them for a horizontal dimension or to one side for a vertical dimension, then click, type the positive distance and press Enter.

让右上圆心落在竖轴上，高度 z = 1.4 in。左圆心为 (−2, 1.05) in，内圈圆心为 (−1, 0.375) in。每个偏移量均按 D，依次点 Origin 和圆心，在两点上方放水平尺寸或在其侧面放竖直尺寸。单击放置，输入距离绝对值并按 Enter。

Source · Course page 16来源 · 课件第 16 页

Check · The lower straight run will be level at z = 0.75 in, the top of the inner circle and bottom of the left circle.

核对 · 下方横直段在 z = 0.75 in，同时经过内圆顶点和左圆底点。

Step 10步骤 10

Connect the circles with tangents用相切直线连接各圆

Choose Line. Draw from the left circle’s upper-left quadrant to the upper-right circle’s upper-left quadrant. Add Tangent between the line and each circle if it did not infer automatically. Draw a horizontal segment from the left circle’s bottom to the inner circle’s top.

选 Line，从左圆的左上象限连到右上圆的左上象限。若未自动相切，分别选择直线和相应圆并添加 Tangent。再从左圆最低点向右连到内圆最高点，画出水平段。

Source · Course page 16来源 · 课件第 16 页

Check · The sloping line touches both circles without crossing either one.

核对 · 斜线与两个圆相切，均未穿入圆内。

Step 11步骤 11

Add both feet and trim the bends添加两脚并修剪弯道

With Line, draw vertically down from the rightmost point on each right-hand bend to the horizontal sketch axis. Both feet end at z = 0. Choose Trim. Click the unused inside arc of each original circle, leaving the continuous route shown. Press Esc and confirm the sketch.

用 Line 从右侧两处弯道各自的最右点竖直向下画到草图水平轴。两脚均终止于 z = 0。选 Trim，在三个原圆上分别点击多余的内侧圆弧，只保留图中的连续路线。按 Esc 并确认草图。

Source · Course page 16来源 · 课件第 16 页

Check · The chain has two free ends and seven connected edges. A surviving closed circle signals a missed trim.

核对 · 这条链有两个自由端和七条连续边。若仍有闭合圆，说明有圆弧尚未修剪。

Step 12步骤 12

Place the round section放置圆形截面

Click Top and Sketch. Choose Center point circle. Click the foot directly below the tall right leg, then click a second point for the radius. Press D, click the circle, place its diameter, enter 0.1 in and press Enter. Confirm the sketch.

单击 Top 和 Sketch。选 Center point circle，单击高右腿正下方的脚端，再单击第二点确定半径。按 D，点圆并放置直径，输入 0.1 in，按 Enter 后确认草图。

Source · Course page 17来源 · 课件第 17 页

Check · The little disk is centered at the end of the taller leg, perpendicular to it.

核对 · 小圆面位于较高右腿的端点中心，并与竖直腿垂直。

Step 13步骤 13

Open the native Sweep打开标准 Sweep

With the route and circular section visible, click the dropdown beside the first solid feature in the toolbar, usually Extrude. Click Sweep. Check that Solid and New are selected.

使路径与圆形截面可见，单击工具栏第一个实体特征（通常为 Extrude）旁的下拉箭头，再单击 Sweep。确认 Solid 和 New 已选中。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The Sweep dialog opens with the profile field active.

核对 · Sweep 面板打开，截面选择栏处于激活状态。

Step 14步骤 14

Select the section sketch选择截面草图

With Faces and sketch regions to sweep active, click Sketch 2 in the feature tree. This is the sketch of the small Ø0.10 in circle at the taller foot.

保持 Faces and sketch regions to sweep 激活，在特征树单击 Sketch 2。这是较高立脚底部直径 0.10 in 小圆的草图。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The profile selection reads Faces of Sketch 2.

核对 · 截面选择栏显示 Faces of Sketch 2。

Step 15步骤 15

Select the full path sketch选择完整路径草图

Click the Sweep path field, then click Sketch 1 in the feature tree. Its connected edges run from one foot, around all three bends, to the other foot.

单击 Sweep path 字段，再单击特征树中的 Sketch 1。该草图的连续边从一个脚端出发，绕过三处弯道，到达另一个脚端。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The path field shows Edges of Sketch 1 and the preview displays the complete rail.

核对 · 路径字段显示 Edges of Sketch 1，预览呈现完整扶手。

Step 16步骤 16

Accept the swept rail保存扫掠生成的扶手

Click the green check at the top of Sweep. Inspect the lower end of the tall leg, the return bend and the short leg in the saved solid.

单击 Sweep 顶部的绿色勾。检查保存后实体的高腿下端、折返弯和短腿。

Source · Recorded in P2 Pool Rail来源 · 录制于 P2 Pool Rail

Check · The feature tree shows Sweep 1 and Parts (1) contains one constant-diameter rail.

核对 · 特征树显示 Sweep 1，Parts (1) 中为一根等直径扶手。

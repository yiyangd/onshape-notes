Cube Transfer方块搬运机构

Transfer a cube from one stand to another. Compare the four preset poses with a positional CAD study in which the carriage, cube, plunger and pad move in four separate Transform features.将方块从一个支座搬运到另一个支座。先查看四个预设姿态，再通过位置运动研究，用四项独立的 Transform 特征控制滑块、方块、推杆与吸盘的移动。

Set up Part 2 Workshop in your Onshape document在自己的 Onshape 文档中添加 Part 2 Workshop
  1. Download the workshop FeatureScript下载 Workshop 的 FeatureScript 源码
  2. Open the downloaded text file and copy its entire contents. In your Onshape practice document, click the + at the bottom left, then click Create Feature Studio.打开下载的文本文件，复制全部内容。在自己的 Onshape 练习文档左下角单击 +，再单击 Create Feature Studio。
  3. Click inside the Feature Studio editor, select all the starter code, and paste the copied text. Click Commit. Open a Part Studio in the same document and click Custom features in this workspace at the right end of the toolbar. Part 2 Workshop should appear in the list.单击 Feature Studio 编辑区，全选初始代码，粘贴复制的文本，再单击 Commit。打开同一文档中的 Part Studio，单击工具栏右端的 Custom features in this workspace，列表中应出现 Part 2 Workshop。

To practice, open your practice document and click + (Insert new tab) > Create Part Studio. Work in this new blank studio. To name it, right-click its tab, choose Rename, type a name, and press Enter. The recorded studio is called P2 Cube Transfer.练习时打开自己的练习文档，单击 +（Insert new tab）> Create Part Studio，在新建的空白工作室中操作。若要命名，右键单击标签，选择 Rename，输入名称后按 Enter。录像中的工作室名为 P2 Cube Transfer。

The second recording is in P2 Cube Transfer - Motion Study. Start in a blank Part Studio there to reproduce the lift, transfer, set-down and release with Translate by XYZ. Each Transform sets a position geometrically. The checkpoints measure clearances and final contact.第二次录制的工作室为 P2 Cube Transfer - Motion Study。可在空白 Part Studio 中使用 Translate by XYZ 复现抬升、转运、放下和松开。每项 Transform 通过几何平移确定一个位置，检查点用于核实间隙与最终接触。

Pages 37–39 pose an open design challenge. All mechanism dimensions are suggested practice values. The 1 in cube travels 6 in between stand centers on a 10 × 2.8 × 0.25 in base. The stand tops are 1.75 in above its underside. The lift and release clearance are 0.45 in.第 37–39 页提出开放式设计挑战。这里的机构尺寸都是建议练习值。边长 1 in 的方块在中心相距 6 in 的两个支座间转运，底板尺寸为 10 × 2.8 × 0.25 in。支座顶面距底板底面 1.75 in，抬升和松开间隙均为 0.45 in。
